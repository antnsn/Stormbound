# Skins

Stormbound comes in three looks. They all work the same way, only the frames look different, so you can match the rest of your UI.

## The three skins

| Skin | Looks like | Good with |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Storm** (default) | Slate storm clouds, an electric blue edge and lightning in the corners. A faint class crest behind the cards, and a rim that pulses slowly while a card needs you. | The default UI, or if you just like the shaman look. |
| **Blizzard** | The game's own gray and silver tooltips, dialogs and buttons. | A mostly default UI where blue would stand out. |
| **Flat** | Thin black edges on dark gray, plain text. | EllesmereUI, ElvUI and other flat UI packs. |

## How to switch

![The Skin page in the options]({{ '/screenshots/options-skin.png' | relative_url }})

1. Type `/storm` and open the **Skin** page.
2. Click the **Skin** button to step through the skins (right-click goes back).
3. Click **Reload now**, or type `/reload` later. The new skin shows after the reload.

The skin is saved with your other Stormbound settings.

## What changes, and what doesn't

A skin changes the frames: colors, borders, fonts, buttons and check boxes in the options window, the cards, the totem bar's box and the travel panel.

Some colors stay the same in every skin:
- red for anything missing or running low,
- the element colors on the [totem bar](totem-bar.html) buttons.

## Good to know

**Why does it need a reload?**
Stormbound builds its frames once, when you log in. Switching in the middle of a session would leave some frames in the old look and some in the new one, so the switch waits for the next reload.

**The class crest** behind the cards, in the options window and on the travel panel is part of the Storm skin. Blizzard and Flat stay plain.
