# Explanations

Hover over a Stormbound card or button and the tooltip says what it is for, in a sentence or two. Turn it off once you know it all.

## What it does

Explanations show in the tooltips of:

- the [totem bar](totem-bar.html) buttons: what totems do, and what left- and right-click do;
- the [shield and Weapon imbue](shield-and-imbue.html) cards: what each shield and imbue is for;
- the [reagent](totem-items-and-ankhs.html) cards: why you need totem items, Ankhs, Fish Oil and Shiny Fish Scales;
- the [travel panel](travel.html) rows: what Astral Recall and the Hearthstone do;
- the [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html) cards.

For example, the totem items card says: "You need the element's totem item in your bags to drop its totems. Lost it? Ask a shaman trainer how to get a new one."

The rest of a tooltip stays when explanations are off: the totem's name, what a click drops, "Click to cast (out of combat)".

## Settings

In `/storm`, page **Skin**, section **General**:

![The Skin page in the options]({{ '/screenshots/options-skin.png' | relative_url }})

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain things in tooltips | On | Turns the explanations on or off. Also `/storm explain`. |

## Good to know

**Cards don't block your clicks.**
A card only takes mouse-over for its tooltip, so you can still click the world under it. A card that offers a cast (shield, imbue) takes the click to cast instead.

**No tooltips while the frames are unlocked.**
Unlocked, the cards are there to be dragged.
