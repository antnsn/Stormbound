# Getting started
{: .no_toc }

Two minutes from install to a shaman with a totem bar and reminders.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## 1. Install it

Pick one:

- **CurseForge app or WowUp:** search for **Stormbound** and click Install.
- **By hand:** download it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/stormbound) and unzip it into your `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` folder, so you get a folder called `Stormbound` there.

Start the game (or type `/reload` if it was already running) and log in on your shaman.

{: .note }
Stormbound only loads on shamans. On another class, `/storm` says so, and the addon is turned off for that character from the next login. The AddOns list can turn it on again.

## 2. Open the options

Type `/storm` in chat, or click the Stormbound button on your minimap.

The options window has one page per feature on the left: **Totems**, **Shield & imbue**, **Reagents**, **Travel**, **Maelstrom**, **Extras** and **Skin**. Most reminders are on by default; Fish Oil and Shiny Fish Scales tracking is optional.

## 3. Drop your totems once

The totem bar has one button per element: Earth, Fire, Water and Air. It shows up once you know a totem.

1. Drop a totem from your spellbook or action bars. The button for its element remembers it.
2. From then on, left-click the button to drop that totem again, and right-click it to remove the totem.
3. Want a button to always drop the same totem? Scroll the mouse wheel over it out of combat, or pick one on the **Totems** page.

![The totem bar]({{ '/screenshots/frames/tbpoint.png' | relative_url }})

More in [Totem bar](features/totem-bar.html).

## 4. Pick your shield

1. In `/storm`, open the **Shield & imbue** page.
2. Click **Shield to keep up** to pick Lightning, Water or Earth Shield.

When your shield runs low or falls off, a card shows up. Click it to cast. Your weapon imbue works the same way. See [Shield and weapon imbue](features/shield-and-imbue.html).

{: .tip }
Set a buff key on the **Extras** page, and one key press casts what those cards ask for. See [Buff key](features/buff-key.html).

## 5. Put the frames where you want them

1. Type `/storm unlock`, Shift-click the minimap button, or click **Unlock frames** at the bottom of the options window.
2. Drag each frame where you want it. Scroll the mouse wheel over a frame to resize it.
3. Click **Lock** on the bar at the top of the screen, or type `/storm lock`.

`/storm reset` puts every frame back where it started. More in [Frames and minimap](features/frames-and-minimap.html).

Using EllesmereUI, ElvUI or another flat UI? Pick the **Flat** skin on the **Skin** page. See [Skins](features/skins.html).

## That's it

Everything else works on its own. When you want to know what a card means or change how something behaves, look it up under [Features](features/).
