## Why Stormbound exists

A shaman keeps track of a lot at once. Four totems with their own timers, a shield that runs out of charges, a weapon imbue that fades, totem items you need in your bags, and Ankhs for Reincarnation. Missing one of them is easy.

Stormbound puts all of that in one addon. It shows your totems on one bar, reminds you when your shield or imbue needs a recast, and warns you before a missing item catches you out. Install it and play: almost everything is on by default.

![The totem bar: one button per element, Earth, Fire, Water and Air]({{ '/screenshots/frames/tbpoint.png' | relative_url }})

## What it does

- **Totem bar.** One button per element with the totem that stands and its time left. Left-click drops it, right-click removes it. [More](features/totem-bar.html)
- **Shield and weapon imbue.** A card when your shield or main-hand imbue is missing or about to run out. Click it to cast. [More](features/shield-and-imbue.html)
- **Totem items and Ankhs.** A card when an Earth, Fire, Water or Air Totem is missing from your bags, and when Ankhs run low. [More](features/totem-items-and-ankhs.html)
- **Buff key.** One key that casts what the shield and imbue cards ask for. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Travel panel.** Press Escape: Astral Recall, Water Breathing, Water Walking and your Hearthstone beside the game menu. [More](features/travel.html)
- **Well-Rested and Well Fed.** A warning and a countdown before they run out. [More](features/well-rested-and-well-fed.html)
- **Explanations, layout and skins.** Tooltips that say what a card or button is for, movable frames, and three looks. [More](features/)

Stormbound is one of the Bound addons, one per class, built on the same core. If you know Lightbound or Chronobound, you already know how the options, frames and skins work.
